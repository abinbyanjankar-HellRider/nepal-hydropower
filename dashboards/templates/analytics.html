What is "Unknown"?

Some projects lack a location, river, developer or type in every source, so the charts above group them as "Unknown". This shows how much that is, why, and lists the projects behind it. Values that were inferred (from coordinates, a same-named DoED project, or words in the project name) are marked on each project page; anything that could not be traced is left Unknown.
